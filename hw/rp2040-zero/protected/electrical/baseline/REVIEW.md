# Minimum carrier Rev A design review

## Connection and power choice

The ordinary DEV build sets the power-off assembly bridge SJ1 to U1. One bridge feeds the module from its selected protected rail. A separately qualified U2-only build can populate the other position. This removes the automatic module supply mux and prevents loss of U1 in the default build from shifting the module or its SD/OLED load onto a suspended laptop. The two bridge positions are mutually exclusive. Keep the selection inaccessible in service and verify the fitted position. The old connector VBUS lands remain tied to module VSYS, which is downstream of SJ1. The baseline M1 assembly has the original receptacle absent and those lands insulated and inaccessible. J2 D+/D− use the module's native lands and original series resistors. The exact Waveshare board and land evidence remain open. [Waveshare circuit](https://files.waveshare.com/upload/4/4c/RP2040_Zero.pdf)

| Interface | Logical four-signal mapping | Role and connector acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J1/U1 | 5 V input, D− open, D+ open, GND | Dedicated 5.10–5.25 V under load, 1.5 A rated supply. Keep the adapter lead captive or keyed in the product so a standard laptop USB port cannot become U1's power source. A four-signal USB-C sink breakout still needs verified separate Rd on CC1 and CC2, an actual rated adapter/cable and safe access control. Four wires alone supply no high-current entitlement. |
| J2/U2 | VBUS in, D−, D+, GND | Laptop-facing USB Device. Four-functional-signal USB-C breakout with correct sink Rd and both plug orientations is the preferred reference. Verify the supplied part's pin order, both D+/D− pairs, shield and CC implementation. A standard USB-B device receptacle can use pins 1 VBUS, 2 D−, 3 D+, 4 GND if it is the selected physical substitute. A USB-A receptacle is for downstream use here. |
| J3/U3 | Protected VBUS out, D− open, D+ open, GND | Standard USB-A, A5/U1 only, 300 mA proposed load envelope. Output is power only, without a USB charging signature. |
| J4/U4 | Protected VBUS out, D−, D+, GND | Standard USB-A PIO Host. GP14 is D+, GP15 is D−. 500 mA configured ceiling on the qualified A supply and 200 mA ceiling on the independently authorized B supply, pending measurement. |

J1 and J2 pin numbers in the schematic are logical four-signal breakout positions, with the conventional USB order as the drawing convention. Measure or obtain the actual module's connector map before assigning a board footprint. On a USB-C version verify Rd, D+/D− orientation mapping and connector pinout on the exact breakout. This paper design has no active CC scanner. A publicly accessible, arbitrary USB-C U1 supply connection that can draw the whole design envelope requires a qualified current-admission approach. USB 2.0 host and Device power are never negotiated merely by connector shape. [USB-IF Type-C model](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/D1T1-2%20-%20USB%20Type-C%20System%20Overview.pdf) · [USB-IF current policy](https://compliance.usb.org/index.asp?Format=Standard&UpdateFile=Policies)

## Laptop and MIDI protection

IC1/2 isolate A5/B5 in reverse and cut faults. U3 has its own reverse-blocking breaker and no path from B5. U4 has a B-only current-limited fail-off branch, a break-before-make selector, and a final reverse-blocking breaker with controlled slew and a separate GP9 enable. Retain two hardware VBUS data gates and connector ESD protection. J2 VBUS has its own sense and protected B feed, with no hard tie to A5, SYS5 or J4. Grounds are common. The code connectivity checks establish net membership. Verify real reverse leakage and transient current, including with the board unpowered, both supplies present, and a forced external J4 voltage. [TPS25947](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf) · [TPS2116](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2116.pdf) · [TPS2553](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2553.pdf) · [TMUXHS221F](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmuxhs221f.pdf)

When AON is valid, GP8 low selects A5 at the U4 mux. GP8 high selects the B path and enables IC7 only with an independent valid B budget. The B branch defaults off on reset. Qualify mux control at startup and brownout where the normal GP8 selector truth table depends on valid AON. Keep GP9 low through source qualification, switching and measured HOST_PRE settle. GP9 low independently drives the final enable off and forces data open. GP20 low reads the data switch command after actual J4 VBUS and dwell. GP18 low corresponds to actual J2 VBUS and its dwell. The readbacks cannot determine available current or analog discharge. GP21 reports a powered IC7 summary fault only. Retire old attachment and grants on fault or loss before a fresh attach. Intentional source changes require GP9 veto first. U4 breaker reset and any power-cycle recovery require deliberate, bounded action. [Schematic](Gridpico-RP2040-Zero-Schematic.pdf)

Output protection also needs evidence with pre-configuration load, simultaneous ramps, USB hot plug, short, detach, grant withdrawal and both supplies. The C62/C63 effective design interval is 170–400 µF. At 170 µF, ideal 5.25 V/10 µF hot plug shares about 292 mV of the 330 mV test allowance before ESR and source behavior. The resistor-only discharge result is a timing screen. J4 can still be energized after GP20 reports data open. Measure actual clearance before fresh attach. Verify U2/U4 full-speed signal integrity and one effective 15 kΩ USB Host termination with current PIO pull behavior. Real native U2 presence, normal attach and BOOTSEL through the exact carrier remain open. ## 3.3 V shared rail and supported accessories

The published Waveshare drawing shows RT9013-33 supplying 3V3 and the onboard GP16 RGB. Richtek lists a 500 mA family rating. That is not a usable accessory budget. In the provisional SOT-23-5 model, 250 °C/W, 45 °C ambient and 5.25 V at VSYS give 0.234 W and about 104 °C junction at 120 mA total 3V3 load. At 180 mA the same screen gives about 133 °C. Use **120 mA total** as a provisional screening ceiling for ordinary DEV pending actual module temperature and current evidence, including regulator layout, load transients, storage writes, OLED startup, RGB and concurrent USB service. The prior illustrative 70 mA module/logic assumption leaves about 50 mA for both peripherals in that screen. The isolated earlier 85 mA SD plus 20 mA OLED assumptions exceed that room. [Waveshare schematic](https://files.waveshare.com/upload/4/4c/RP2040_Zero.pdf) · [Richtek RT9013](https://www.richtek.com/en/product-specifications/rt90/rt9013) · [Richtek DS9013 thermal data](https://atta.szlcsc.com/upload/public/pdf/source/20150504/1457707282459.pdf)

Fit qualified 3.3 V SD and OLED directly to P1.21 and the common 3.3 V rail. Check the purchased card's peak write/start draw, its exact socket or breakout pin map, the OLED supply/pinout/onboard pull-ups, and local rail stability with accessory bypass capacitance. Defer a complete unqualified interface, including its power and signal connections. Power the board off for accessory or external-peer wiring. UART and external pixel outputs are 3.3 V data with ground and no 5 V power. A separately powered 5 V strip needs compatible external signal translation. The direct, unbuffered 3.3 V accessory path accepts local Pico exposure to miswiring and removes switched/hot-plug accessory behavior. In a U2-fed module build, fit only loads that meet USB attach, configured and suspend limits, including ROM/reset. The default 2.5 mA USB 2.0 suspend ceiling is measured at J2. [USB-IF suspend requirement](https://compliance.usb.org/index.asp?UpdateFile=Electrical)

## Three-unit and firmware boundaries

The paper chain is PC to A U2 as a native link, then A U4 to B U2 and B U4 to C U2 as routed MIDI hops. For a one-supply trial concept, SJ1 on A is U1 and SJ1 on B/C is U2. At 125 mA local per unit, 100 mΩ loop cables and a 5.10 V loaded head, middle U4 screens at 4.947 V. With a 4.75 V head it screens at 4.597 V. B needs a measured and independently admitted aggregate current of at least its local load and C's draw. The illustrative 250 mA total exceeds the installed 100 mA configuration. C also requires an independent current admission under B. These steady-state numbers do not close transient, protection selectivity, default-current or resume/suspend cases. Keep chain and powered qualification on hold. A U2-fed module profile is conditional even without U4 attached. [Power screen](power-budget.txt) · [Installed firmware evidence](/tmp/gridpico-rev-a-firmware-evidence.0IuPzz/RESULT.md)

Default U1-backed module service keeps SD/OLED and no automatic failover. U1 loss stops the module even if the PC remains attached. The U2-to-U4 path is retained with valid B authorization. Source coordination, native session state, actual USB role/current decisions, approved descriptor and USB Host enforcement remain Assembly/Architect integration dependencies. PCB placement and the actual purchased connectors, module, microSD, OLED and U1 adapter remain open. This four-sheet bundle is a paper review with an editable net source.

## Pinout lock and simplified U1-only option

The schematic provides exact logical pins for the selected reference mapping. The physical connector and module pinout remains provisional. The part evidence needed before lock is the module's order identifier and PCB revision or clear identifying images, native D+/D− and VBUS land continuity, the order code or connector breakout schematic and physical pad order, and the attached cable/adapter type and current rating. USB-C upstream candidates also need both Rd terminations and both data orientations confirmed. Power qualification needs the selected SD or breakout's cold/write peak, the OLED's startup/operating draw and pull-up arrangement, and module 3.3 V rail and thermal margin with both loads. No purchased-part sheets were available in the historical review or current design inputs.

A reviewed U1-only U4 option could remove the B host branch and host source selector while retaining the final U4 switch, reverse block, short protection, controlled rise and VBUS gating. That option gives up U2-powered U4 and the one-supply chain. Each unit supplying a downstream link needs its own U1 feed. The reference schematic preserves protected U2-to-U4 under published brief 8acb1af. [One-page comparison](carrier-comparison.pdf)
