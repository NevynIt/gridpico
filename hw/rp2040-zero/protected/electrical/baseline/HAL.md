## Qualification blockers and deltas

- **Independent U2 grant, sheets 1–3.** GP8 must remain off unless an actual U2 aggregate allocation covers the entire B draw, including base load under U2-fed SJ1. Default/unknown CC, raw VBUS good, a configured-but-100 mA descriptor, and the eFuse threshold cannot establish this. The installed normal/recovery images offer 100 mA; the conditional middle-unit budget is 250 mA, and a 125 mA tail also exceeds that declaration. Clear a grant on suspend/reset/detach or lost authorization. Exercise J2 input protection at legal high VBUS too. IC2 R22/R23 set nominal OVLO to 5.376 V, so a USB-legal 5.50 V can shut off B5; with default SJ1 and healthy U1, raw VBUS can still drive GP18's data-gate indication. The U2-only or B-power variant cannot assume all otherwise valid USB sources sustain it. [USB-IF current and VBUS rules](https://compliance.usb.org/index.asp?UpdateFile=Electrical) · [USB Type-C](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/USB%20Type-C%20Spec%20R2.0%20-%20August%202019.pdf)
- **U2 default-current suspend, sheet 1.** SJ1 default removes the Rev A automatic SYS5-to-laptop fallback. It does not itself close the 2.5 mA USB 2.0 suspend limit. Measure total J2 current, J2 data/presence and SYS5/AON with U1 alive, during U1 loss, while IC7 is denied, and separately under U2-only SJ1 in application, reset and ROM/BOOTSEL. Account for the IC2 quiescent/disabled currents, dividers, B branch leakage and pull-ups. A failed U2-only mode remains excluded. [USB-IF suspend ECN](https://compliance.usb.org/index.asp?UpdateFile=Electrical)
- **U4 dynamic margin, sheets 2–3.** IC13/R132/R133 screen at about 4.603 V worst rising and 4.566 V worst falling with the inherited ±0.25% resistor and ±0.25 µA leakage allowances. A 330 mV droop from the 4.75 V settled floor reaches 4.42 V. The gate may reset during a permitted voltage excursion, and C132 delays release only. Require measured loaded dwell/droop and state whether the intended profile retains data or reattaches after such a dip. C62 Panasonic 16SVPF270M and C63 1 µF remain conditional on total effective capacitance/ESR/temperature/age. At 170 µF, ideal hot-plug already uses 292 of 330 mV. R66-only reconnect screening is 1.50–3.54 s nominal; require actual decay or a qualified discharge path. R67 100 Ω is in series with C61 39 nF as recommended. The fitted 400 µF slew screen is 37.3 mA, so 200 mA concurrent output plus ramp and 2 mA allowance gives 239.3 mA against IC7's 220.8 mA minimum. Constrain full-load overlap or prove safe sequencing/dwell. Check one effective PIO 15 kΩ termination and full-speed signal integrity. [USB-IF drop/droop](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/USB20_32_BC12_Drop_Droop_1_4_1.pdf) · [TPS25947](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf)
- **Fault containment and chain, sheets 1–2.** The 125 mA local screen plus the IC7 upper 292.1 mA gives 417.1 mA, only 7.9 mA below the 425 mA IC2 data-sheet reference at 6.65 kΩ. R21 is actually 6.57 kΩ ±0.1%. Obtain fitted limit/timing and transient bounds before claiming selectivity; GP21 can lose power before reporting or read high when B is unpowered. Qualify both downstream and input-first faults and recovery. The chain's 4.947 V middle U4 is a conditional steady screen at a 5.10 V head, 100 mΩ cable loops and 125 mA/unit. At 4.75 V head, middle U4 screens at 4.597 V, below the 4.75 V settled floor regardless of detector. Recheck both hop load, transient and upstream authorization margins for the selected hardware. [TPS25947](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf) · [TPS2553](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2553.pdf)
- **Local load and connector acceptance, sheet 4.** The RT9013 120 mA total rail budget is a planning screen (about 104 °C modeled junction at 45 °C ambient). The illustrative 70 mA local plus 85 mA SD and 20 mA OLED exceeds it. Select the exact module/regulator lot, measure SD/OLED/LED and startup/write peaks and rail/temperature under USB load, or leave the unqualified whole interface disconnected. Direct 3.3 V accessory wiring provides no hot-plug or powered-peer isolation. Qualify the optional OLED pull-ups and J1/J2 footprints before layout release.
