## Exact power paths

- **Sheets 1–2.** IC1 and IC2 are independent TPS259474LRPWR stages from U1_RAW to A5 and U2_RAW to B5 respectively. A5 is the sole supply for IC4.5 → U3, IC6.5 → U4, and J8.3. D10 is retained on U1_RAW. IC9 TPS2116DRLR uses B5 at VIN1 and MODE and senses B5 through R901/R902 at PR1. A5 is VIN2 fallback and IC9 feeds SYS5 only. There is no A5/B5 tie, IC7, IC8 or U2 outlet branch. With both sources usable, B supplies the module and U1 supplies the outlets and J8. With U1 alone A also supplies the module. With U2 alone the three A5 load supplies are off apart from residual charge and unintended injection. With both off no forward source is intended. The eFuses are the raw-connector reverse barriers. Require assembled leakage and transfer evidence with the inactive input driven low and floating, both source orders and drop/recovery. These limits do not cover a raw short before IC1/IC2, which needs the exact sources and cables to provide protection. U3/U4 retain individual output eFuses. J8 is directly on A5 behind IC1, so a J8 short can drop the shared rail and a forced J8 supply can energize the A rail and outlets. [TI eFuse reverse blocking and fault behavior](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf) · [TI mux priority mode and transfer](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2116.pdf)
- **IC9 senses available voltage.** The sealed B5 priority screen is 3.818–4.503 V. A physically unplugged cable normally removes B5, but an off or weak USB host, a fault or IC2 cutoff can take B5 below priority with the cable still attached. U1 can take SYS5 in either case. A suspended host can keep valid B5 and remain the selected source. Weak B5 near the threshold, break-before-make droop and restart need fitted validation. IC2's screened rising OVLO of 5.612–5.834 V permits B5 above IC9's 5.5 V recommended maximum before cutoff. Qualify both source voltage/transient envelopes at the mux. [TI TPS2116](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2116.pdf) · [Power review](/tmp/gridpico-minimal-direct-rgb.v3kQ4E/POWER-REVIEW.md)
- **U2 fitted current.** U2 supplies SYS5, the module and its 3.3 V SD/OLED loads. Account for the regulator, onboard LED, about 120 µA nominal B5 priority divider, other losses and any GPIO export. The recorded Pico7 advertises 100 mA. The 125 mA local screen already exceeds that and the nominal ~507 mA IC2 breaker gives no entitlement to USB current. Measure total J2 current using exact module revision and accessory/card fit through attach/inrush, default and configured operation, SD/OLED peak activity, source handover, cold reset, ROM and settled electrical USB suspend. USB 2.0 suspend allows 2.5 mA. Qualify actual module 3.3 V dropout and thermal margin separately. [USB-IF USB 2.0 suspend and inrush guidance](https://compliance.usb.org/index.asp?UpdateFile=Electrical) · [Installed Pico7 descriptor evidence](/tmp/gridpico-rev-a-firmware-evidence.0IuPzz/RESULT.md) · [Waveshare module schematic](https://files.waveshare.com/upload/4/4c/RP2040_Zero.pdf)

## Direct data and J8 fit holds

- **Sheets 3–4, J2 native USB.** J2 D± go directly to M1's former data lands and the module's existing 27 Ω series resistors. U2_RAW is separate from the insulated former VBUS lands on SYS5. An accessible stock connector or a connection to those VBUS lands would bypass the intended isolation. Exact module/connector fit, both CC/Rd terms and data orientations remain open. With U1 driving SYS5 and a cabled PC VBUS off, the data lines are still physically connected. The RP2040 ROM explicitly overrides VBUS detection and enables the pull-up; the recorded normal firmware also forces VBUS present. Treat U1-powered BOOTSEL with J2 VBUS absent as a concrete upstream D+ drive path. A VBUS eFuse cannot address it. Any proposed connected use needs a supported power-state policy or fitted back-voltage evidence for VBUS and D± through off-host, attach/removal and normal/reset/ROM operation. [RP2040 ROM source](https://github.com/raspberrypi/pico-bootrom-rp2040/blob/ef22cd8ede5bc007f81d7f2416b48db90f313434/usb_device_tiny/usb_device.c#L1313-L1320) · [USB-IF upstream back-voltage test](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/USB-IFTestProc1_3.pdf)
- **Sheet 3, J4 PIO USB.** Direct GP14/15 paths retain 22 Ω series resistors and 15 kΩ pull-downs. The exact firmware and external parts must establish one effective host termination profile. IC10–15 and D101/D121 are absent. U4 VBUS off does not open D±. Bound direct data injection both ways with peers independently powered/off and in reset, plus data-line ESD or VBUS-to-data exposure for the allowed host, cable and equipment. The retained IC6 protects the VBUS path and does not qualify the direct data pair. [Minimal netlist](/tmp/gridpico-minimal-direct-rgb.v3kQ4E/pin-netlist.csv)
- **Sheet 4, J8 RGB.** J8.1 is common GND, J8.2 is GP17 from M1.P2.1 through existing R81 (100 Ω), and J8.3 is A5. The GP17 nets contain only those terminals. IC16/C161/R162 are absent from BOM and pin/net export. R81 provides series resistance and no level shift, off-state isolation or reverse block. Require a keyed load-only fit with exact strip input thresholds at its actual 5 V rail, supply range and peak load, exact cable/input waveform and no external power or reverse data at J8. RP2040's 2.62 V minimum VOH at 3.3 V IOVDD is specified at its stated drive/load conditions. A hypothetical 0.7×5 V input threshold would need 3.5 V and is unsuitable on the drawn direct path. Verify U2-only GP17 drive into an unpowered strip, the reverse sequencing case with an unpowered Pico, and A5 loss/ramp for clamp injection and phantom power within both parts' limits and the J2 budget. No strip or connector fit is currently qualified.
