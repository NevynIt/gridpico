## Laptop isolation and exact missing input

The drawing's `U4_VBUS_ISLAND` has only J4's open carrier pad and the hub cable-side upstream VBUS. There is no drawn DC VBUS link from that island to `U2_VBUS`. Stock M1 P1.23 remains tied internally to native U2 VBUS. The drawing assumes no breakout bridge, leakage path, or extra external wire at assembly. Grounds are shared. The native and PIO data pairs are separate wiring, but J4 D± connect through only R1/R2 to GP14/GP15. Those series resistors do not disconnect an unpowered GPIO. A hub driving data into a de-energized module is an unqualified rail-injection path; neither absence nor magnitude of current at the laptop-facing VBUS/data pins is established. The retained traffic receipts ran with the Pico powered and do not test that state. Required subsequent evidence is exact de-energized continuity and same-combination hub-paced attach/isolation evidence, including the hub powered with the PC physically disconnected and a check for voltage/current on upstream J4, exposed native U2 VBUS and D± and M1 5 V/3V3 rails. No such measurement was found. [Variant drawing and fit gate](/tmp/gridpico-bench-variant.HQxjSj/README.md:11)
