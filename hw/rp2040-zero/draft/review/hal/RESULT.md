## Native U2 and independent USB-C inputs

Waveshare shows stock connector VBUS, module VSYS and P1.23 on one net. The carrier netlist sends SYS5 to P1.23 and leaves the former VBUS lands at SYS5. J2 U2_RAW stays on the protected B input and independent sense path. Therefore removing the stock receptacle, insulating its live former VBUS contacts and routing only the native data lands across the carrier switch are assembly requirements. Document purchased module revision, removal process, postwork continuity/isolation, no mating connection to the old lands, short differential route preserving onboard series resistors, and full-speed/recovery enumeration with J2 alone and with U1 also present. Leave BOOTSEL/RESET accessible. [Waveshare schematic](https://files.waveshare.com/upload/4/4c/RP2040_Zero.pdf) · [RP2040 datasheet](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2040/rp2040-datasheet.pdf)

Separate UFP TUSB320LAI controllers, separate CC1/CC2 per port, separate VBUS_DET and distinct addresses 0x47/0x67 match the topology. No PD contract exists. Default/unknown CC advertisement does not grant arbitrary current. U2 bus-power use must cover initial attach, ROM, configuration, suspend, loss of U1 and detach. The existing Pico7 device descriptor declares 100 mA, which cannot authorize the modeled 360 mA middle-unit draw from a standard USB-A host. Current firmware does not implement these board grants. Verify attach/current observations, admitted bus-power descriptor and disconnect/downgrade behavior before enabling U3/U4/peripheral branches. U1 should require a qualified source/Rp for its high-power profile. [TUSB320LAI](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tusb320lai.pdf) · [USB Type-C](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/USB%20Type-C%20Spec%20R2.0%20-%20August%202019.pdf)

## Power path, cutoff and inrush

U3 has one steady-state feed, A5 through a source-local enable and TPS259474. Through the drawn topology B5 does not sustain U3, and TPS259474 provides reverse blocking with the branch on, off or unpowered. External VBUS, transition charge and finite leakage still need tests on U3, both input jacks and the two U4 mux states. TPS2116 break-before-make and upstream branch switches support protected selection, with finite reverse-detection delay, turn-off transients and breakover droop. Brief A/B dropouts, both grants, brownout, faults, rapid unplug and externally driven U3/U4 must be included. No glitchless switchover claim. U3's 300 mA applies to a qualified load independently of USB charging, as its data pins are open. [TPS25947](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf) · [TPS2116](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2116.pdf)

IC7 TPS2553-1 and 105 kΩ ±1% give 221/254/292 mA minimum/nominal/maximum screening thresholds. The 200 mA continuous U4-B envelope fits below the minimum. A 360 mA three-board middle total leaves just 65 mA before the 425 mA IC2 minimum, and 180 + 292 = 472 mA means upstream-before-branch fault is possible. The branch latches off on sustained overcurrent. With 180 µF assumed effective, the owner's 36 mA nominal and 65.5 mA screening-high U4-ramp increments give 216–245.5 mA if the 180 mA tail load overlaps. The high case crosses the minimum branch threshold. Start branches in order with actual settle criteria and measure each current/rail waveform with local load, capacitive load and cable variations. A steady-state current figure alone cannot qualify startup. IC7 FAULT is open, and VBUS-good cannot identify switch fault cause. Confirm bounded fault handling and reconnect behavior. [TPS2553](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2553.pdf) · [TPS25947](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf)

The owner's 5.10 V / 180 mA-per-unit assumed chain gives head U4 4.978 V, middle U2 4.942 V, middle U4 4.861 V and tail U2 4.843 V. At 4.75 V head U1, middle U4 drops to 4.511 V in the same screening model; head U4 is about 4.628 V. The 4.989 V calculated minimum head input is only the static middle U4 floor under the assumed resistances, before droop/guardbands. U4 at 100 mA or above needs 4.75 V at the output connector for the relevant embedded-host envelope. At least 120 µF at J4 does not itself prove 330 mV droop: ideal 120 µF sharing with 10 µF initially discharged drops about 365 mV from 4.75 V before ESR/source dynamics; the actual USB test must establish it. Qualify actual cable, source minimum at head input, per-unit load, capacitance tolerance/DC bias/ESR, hot plug, SC and thermal transients. Unrestricted chaining requires changed power architecture or a narrower published envelope. [USB-IF drop/droop requirements](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/USB20_32_BC12_Drop_Droop_1_4_1.pdf)

### Data gate voltage

With IC13 EA46, its 4.63 V nominal falling threshold, +2% accuracy and +1.5% hysteresis give a 4.793 V worst rising sense threshold even before R132 and clamp leakage. The RESET output drives R131 = 100 kΩ. For near-rail RESET high and R132 = 4.7 kΩ, that load adds as much as 225 mV of drop at this trip point, plus IC supply current and Zener leakage. This screening corner puts the near-rail loaded-equivalent rising threshold near 5.02 V raw and the near-rail loaded hold threshold near 4.95 V raw, before clamp leakage. This is a screening estimate, not an oscillation prediction. At a minimum, the threshold alone precludes guaranteed enable at the modeled 4.75 V output floor. The same analysis on U2's EA43 gives 4.535 V worst rising before losses, with screening near 4.75 V closed-loop raw. Set and bound both thresholds across cable loss, tolerance and release delay; preserve positive U2-absent gating and U4 enable override while doing so. [TLV809E](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv809e.pdf) · [TMUXHS221F](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmuxhs221f.pdf)

## GPIO and secondary interfaces

RP2040 function table matches UART0 GP0/1, I²C1 GP2/3, I²C0 GP4/5, SPI1 GP10–13, PIO USB DP/DM on GP14/15, onboard pixel GP16 and external pixel data GP17. The grant/sense and peripheral enable pins do not collide in this map. Existing PIO USB calls gpio_pull_down on GP14/15. The external 15 kΩ therefore adds in parallel; qualify effective termination and native/PIO FS signal integrity through both switch paths. Assign runtime PIO/SM/DMA/IRQ resources before adding RGB or SD DMA. Buffered UART is 3.3 V CMOS with shared ground; external RGB is data only and needs its own correctly level-shifted power path for a 5 V strip. SPI buffer split rails and OLED hot-swap buffer HIZ topology match their pin maps. Qualify effective card rail and detect/removal policy (CD not wired), exact OLED orientation/pin order/pull-ups/current, and no 5 V peripherals on these headers. [RP2040 datasheet](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2040/rp2040-datasheet.pdf) · [TXU0304](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/txu0304.pdf) · [TCA9511A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tca9511a.pdf)
