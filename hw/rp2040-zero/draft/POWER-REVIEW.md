# RP2040-Zero carrier power review

**Engineering draft.** The schematic specifies electrical connections and design envelopes. The carrier uses a Waveshare RP2040-Zero, two USB-C sink inputs, U3 power only from U1, and a protected, source-selected U4 USB host. All grounds are common.

## Connections and isolation

| Port | Data and CC | Power |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **U1 / J1** | USB-C UFP/sink. TUSB320 supplies separate Rd on CC1/CC2, including dead-battery termination, and reads advertised current. Data and SBU open. | 5 V only through IC1 reverse-blocking eFuse to A5. No PD request. |
| **U2 / J2** | USB-C UFP/sink with its own TUSB320. Ganged D+/D− pairs reach the native RP2040 PHY through IC10 when real U2 VBUS is present. | 5 V only through IC2 to B5. The computer can power local logic and, with a separate grant, U4. |
| **U3 / J3** | USB-A power accessory. Pins 2 and 3 open. No BC 1.2 signature. | IC4 from A5 only. Reverse blocking, latched protection and a separate permit. Proposed 300 mA load envelope. |
| **U4 / J4** | USB-A PIO host, GP14 D+ / GP15 D−. Separate data switch, 22 Ω series and 15 kΩ connector-side pulldowns. Data opens without both VBUS good and port enable. | A5 via IC5 or B5 via lower-current IC7, selected by IC8. Final IC6 provides soft start, fault cutoff and reverse blocking. |

The Waveshare schematic shows the stock native connector VBUS contacts hard-wired to **VSYS**, P1.23 (the pad marked 5 V) and the RT9013 input. The proposed assembly removes that receptacle and uses a **new carrier-mounted J2**. Only the old D_P and D_N lands connect to J2 through the data switch, with the original 27 Ω series parts retained. The former VBUS lands remain on SYS5 and must be physically insulated. The old CC networks have no external contact. The native switch goes high-impedance when real J2 power is absent or the board is off. Firmware will also need to observe the sensed native session boundary. The existing TinyUSB port forces VBUS present by default. Validate enumeration, detach and BOOTSEL over J2 on the revised design. [Waveshare schematic](https://files.waveshare.com/upload/4/4c/RP2040_Zero.pdf) · [TUSB320LAI](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tusb320lai.pdf) · [TMUXHS221F](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmuxhs221f.pdf) · [RP2040 datasheet](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2040/rp2040-datasheet.pdf)

Two protected rails enter the SYS5 priority mux. Each U4 and optional peripheral branch receives its own source grant before its mux. When U1 disappears, SYS5 can fall back to U2 while U4 and the SD/OLED load require their respective U2 grants. Source-local MOS enables prevent powered GPIOs from sourcing an absent input. The U3/4 final eFuses block a forced output from powering an input or the other source. UART/RGB use power-off buffers and ground-referenced ESD protection. A powered carrier still needs a power-off-safe external receiver or common powered operation. A 5 V strip uses its own power and a power-off-safe level shifter at the strip end. The OLED header exports a carrier-supplied rail. The card and display are local loads without a separate supply. Reverse blocking still has finite leakage and switching response. ## Current and inrush

**Sourced limits.** USB-IF lists 500 mA as the standard USB 2.0 downstream maximum and 4.75 V minimum for the PC and high-power embedded-host test cases. Default legacy A-to-C cables advertise default current via 56 kΩ Rp. A recognized 1.5 A or 3 A Type-C current level is distinct from default current or a USB configuration grant. The TPS25947 data sheet gives 28.2 mΩ typical and 45 mΩ maximum under its stated conditions. At the listed 6.65 kΩ test setting the threshold is 425–575 mA. The TPS2116 lists 40 mΩ typical. The TPS2553 DBV lists 135 mΩ maximum over its stated temperature range. Its data-sheet fit at 105 kΩ ±1% screens at about 221/254/292 mA minimum/typical/maximum. [USB-IF current policy](https://compliance.usb.org/index.asp?UpdateFile=Policies) · [VBUS drop and droop](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/USB20_32_BC12_Drop_Droop_1_4_1.pdf) · [USB Type-C legacy cable definition](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/USB%20Type-C%20Spec%20R2.0%20-%20August%202019.pdf) · [TPS25947](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf) · [TPS2116](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2116.pdf) · [TPS2553](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2553.pdf)

**Assumed budgets.** Allow 70 mA always-on for the module, restrained pixel and control logic. Allow 110 mA optional, illustrated by an 85 mA peak selected microSD, 20 mA OLED and 5 mA buffers. The active local target is **180 mA** over load peaks. U1 needs detected 1.5 A or greater at 5 V for auxiliary grants. The concurrent U1 plan totals 180 + 300 + 500 = **980 mA**, with a nominal IC1 breaker setting of 1.25 A. Verify the actual breaker bounds against both startup transients and the authorized supply. Legacy U2 targets the 100 mA pre-configuration envelope with discretionary loads off. Its U4 path is limited to 200 mA after an independent upstream grant, and the local budget plus the calculated high limiter threshold totals about 472 mA steady. The input eFuse may latch earlier on overload. The B-powered port requires a bounded peripheral list. A 500 mA peripheral is outside its envelope.

**Inrush and cutover.** Each raw VBUS starts with about 1 µF plus sense/ESD parasitics. The large capacitors charge behind controlled switches. At U4, specify 150 µF with at least 120 µF effective, charged by IC6 after HOST_PRE settles. With 10 nF on DVDT, the data-sheet law screens at about 36 mA for 180 µF nominally and 66 mA at the estimated fast corner. A connected downstream unit may add up to 100 mA before configuration. Verify this combined pulse against the B path and sequence the stages. Measure input capacitance, droop, inrush, and suspend current separately. For source changes, the controller drops U4 data and output enable before switching, then brings the port back as a fresh attach. The 4.7 kΩ bleeder and 180 µF give about 1.6 s no-load decay from 5.25 V to 0.8 V. Verify actual discharge or design an active discharger. [TPS25947 soft-start equations](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf)

## Three-unit chain

Qualify one topology first. Head **A** gets a 5.10–5.25 V supply measured at U1 under load and valid 1.5 A or greater advertisement. Connect **A U4 → B U2**, then **B U4 → C U2**, with verified USB data cables. Keep U3 and the tail host power off. B needs a configured upstream allocation of 500 mA to cover itself and C. C's offered configuration must fit the 200 mA B port budget and its active local load stays within 180 mA. Configure or provision distinct head, middle and tail power roles. A fixed 500 mA USB descriptor for every unit would exceed B's tail budget. U3-to-U1 over an ordinary A-to-C cable provides default CC advertisement and no configuration grant. That power-only connection does not qualify a three-active-unit chain.

The model uses 55 mΩ per eFuse, 75 mΩ per mux, 135 mΩ for IC7, 20 mΩ input and 40 mΩ output board/contact allowance per node, and **100 mΩ loop maximum per cable**. At 180 mA per unit, the head input carries 540 mA, the first cable 360 mA and the second 180 mA. Calculated volts are **5.100 → 4.978 at A U4 → 4.942 at B U2 → 4.861 at B U4 → 4.843 at C U2 → 4.812 at C SYS5**. B U4 has 111 mV static margin over 4.75 V. With 4.75 V at the head, B U4 calculates to 4.511 V. Longer cords, generic hubs, unknown peripherals, or lower source voltage need separate qualification. ## Board designer and reviewer checks

- Check exact module revision, pad geometry, connector land strength and enclosure insulation after receptacle removal. Verify both Type-C orientations, ESD and common-ground returns, external backdrive under each power combination, USB FS impedance, switch loss and PIO termination. The profile should suppress the PIO internal weak pulls when the 15 kΩ external pair is fitted. U2's proposed VBUS supervisor can refuse attachment at the low-voltage corner. Set and validate the actual threshold and transient margin before qualification. [Pico-PIO-USB reference](https://github.com/sekigon-gonnoc/Pico-PIO-USB)
- Verify cold, hot and fault thresholds, USB attach/suspend current, U4 soft-start, realistic cable loss, card write bursts, OLED charge-pump startup, simultaneous outputs and regulator heating. At 5.25 V the assumed 70 mA module load incurs about 0.137 W ideal LDO loss. The peripheral LDO at 110 mA incurs about 0.215 W. An IC current rating does not establish safe dissipation on the module or carrier. [Richtek RT9013](https://www.richtek.com/en/Products/Linear%20Regulator/Single%20Output%20Regulator/RT9013.aspx) · [TLV755P](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv755p.pdf)
- Qualify the GPIO power policy, grants, fault sequencing and USB descriptors before enabling auxiliary loads. Any U2 bus-only suspend-current issue remains open until measured on the selected implementation. Board design still needs footprint/layout, component cross-check, ERC, prototype measurement, and the nominated HAL electrical and Architecture topology reviews. This is a design review artifact.
