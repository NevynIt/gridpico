# Three-unit current, fault and startup hold

This companion applies to schematic draft manifest **25dbfc048bc370898640e842a84f8a03d5716f48b2742c6a44791b48a75bb62c**. It supplements the existing U4 voltage and data-enable hold with Linux HAL's current and USB power review. The sealed design and first hold retain their original bytes. [Existing voltage hold](ELECTRICAL-HOLD.md)

| Case | Exact screening result | Open requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Middle B steady load | Middle device 180 mA + tail 180 mA = **360 mA** into IC2. The input breaker has a data-sheet minimum threshold of **425 mA** at the fitted 6.65 kΩ test setting. The resulting nominal budget has **65 mA** before any omitted tolerance or startup load. | Measure aggregate current on the actual B assembly, including auxiliary transients and the specified load envelope. |
| B downstream fault | IC7 nominal-corner model screens up to **292 mA** before its branch trips. Together with the local 180 mA target, IC2 could see **472 mA**. This exceeds IC2's 425 mA minimum. | IC7-before-IC2 fault selectivity is unproven. Qualify threshold and response-time coordination over tolerances and fault profiles, or explicitly design for loss of the middle unit on a downstream fault and qualify that recovery. |
| U4 startup | Concurrent active tail **180 mA** plus **65.5 mA** estimated U4 ramp current totals **245.5 mA**, exceeding the **220.8 mA** minimum IC7 screening threshold. If the tail holds its verified pre-configuration envelope of at most 100 mA through the ramp, the corresponding estimate is **165.5 mA**. | Sequence HOST_PRE, U4 rise, tail configuration and auxiliary enable. Verify the actual settling, hot-plug/inrush, reset and reconnect cases so the full tail load cannot coincide with an unqualified ramp. |
| Upstream authorization | Exact installed Pico7 source advertises a **100 mA bus-powered** USB configuration. The TinyUSB macro encodes that in 2 mA units, producing bMaxPower 50. | Middle B requires a new configuration the head host can actually admit for the measured total and appropriate margin, or a separately documented valid supply authorization. The breaker setting and legacy default Rp supply neither authorization. C still needs a configuration within the B port limit. |
| Existing voltage hold | A **5.10 V** head input screens to **4.861 V** at the middle U4, within the current IC13 reset-divider concern. | Sequencing, a revised descriptor and current margin do not close the voltage and data-enable hold. |

The values are arithmetic or published component limits rather than measurements on a board. Keep the draft for design review only until Hardware Design and the electrical reviewer close the holds.
